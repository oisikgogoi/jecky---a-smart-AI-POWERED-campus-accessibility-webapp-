Sample study note
Engineering Physics: Introduction to wave optics

These illustrative notes are provided as sample content only.

Interference
- Interference results when waves overlap and combine.
- Constructive interference occurs when waves reinforce one another.
- Destructive interference occurs when waves reduce one another's amplitude.
- In Young's double-slit experiment, the fringe pattern depends on wavelength,
  slit separation, and distance to the screen.

Diffraction
- Diffraction describes the spreading of waves around an obstacle or through
  an aperture.
- The effect is most noticeable when the aperture size is comparable to the
  wavelength.
