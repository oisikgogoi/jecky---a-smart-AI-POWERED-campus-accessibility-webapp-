Engineering Mathematics I: Limits and derivatives

These illustrative notes are provided as sample content only.

Limits
- A limit describes the value a function approaches as its input approaches a point.
- For a polynomial, direct substitution can be used to evaluate a finite limit.
- When direct substitution gives an indeterminate form, simplify the expression
  before evaluating it.

Derivatives
- The derivative represents the instantaneous rate of change of a function.
- Power rule: d(x^n)/dx = n*x^(n-1).
- Sum rule: differentiate each term separately.
- Product and quotient rules apply to products and ratios of differentiable
  functions.

Use your course text and instructor guidance for complete definitions and
worked examples.
